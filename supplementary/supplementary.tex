\section{Registered Calibration and Support Protocols}
\label{sec:supp_protocols}

\noindent\textbf{Grade display.}
Display Grade $k$ corresponds to native label ID $k-1$; stored labels and score-column order are unchanged.

\noindent\textbf{Calibration grids.}
Tables~\ref{tab:supp_bias_adience}--\ref{tab:supp_bias_koniq} give the complete candidate grid for every primary configuration, mask, and seed.
Each calibrated method uses its own largest feasible offset.
The seen-validation nMAE budget is uncalibrated Adapted nMAE plus $\delta=0.005$.
The negative-infinity boundary removes missing-grade predictions.
An empty feasible set is recorded as blocked, rather than assigned a replacement offset.

\noindent\textbf{Support denominator.}
The 10\% and 20\% levels refer to each target grade's quota in a balanced, pre-mask pool of 256 images drawn from the same-seed training-only member union.
Remainder quotas follow ascending native-grade order.
For a positive fraction $r$ and target-grade quota $q_k$, the retained count is $\max(1,\lceil r q_k\rceil)$; 0\% retains no target-grade images.

\noindent\textbf{Fixed-total protocol.}
G2a covers Adience and HCI, and G2b covers FairFace and KonIQ.
The total remains 256 images by removing seen-grade images in round-robin grade order as target-grade images are added.

\noindent\textbf{Fixed-seen protocol.}
G3 covers Adience and HCI.
The pre-mask seen-grade images remain identical at all three support levels, while target-grade images are added.
Its zero-support comparison uses its own matched zero-support run.
